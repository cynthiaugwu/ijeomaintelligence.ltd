Content & Media Assistant

Contract · Remote · Nigeria

About Ijeoma

Ijeoma Intelligence develops safety intelligence for people and organisations operating in complex and changing risk environments.

Initially focused on Nigeria, we are building systems designed for environments where risk, movement and connectivity can change quickly.

The Role

The Content & Media Assistant supports how Ijeoma communicates its work to public and professional audiences.

The role combines digital content, media support, community engagement and selected field-based content production.

It suits someone who understands how to translate serious subjects into clear, useful and engaging communication without losing credibility.

What You’ll Work On

  • Create and prepare digital content across Ijeoma’s public communication channels.
  • Adapt content appropriately for different audiences and platforms.
  • Support educational and editorial content related to safety, movement and risk awareness.
  • Assist with interviews, field content and other public-facing media activities where required.
  • Support community engagement and day-to-day management of Ijeoma’s digital presence.
  • Maintain organised content schedules and support the wider marketing and communications team.

What We’re Looking For

  • Strong understanding of digital and social media content.
  • Ability to shoot and edit short-form video.
  • Strong spoken and written English.
  • Good judgement when communicating sensitive or serious subjects.
  • Comfortable conducting interviews and working with members of the public where required.
  • Based in Nigeria.
  • Able to work independently and manage assigned work reliably.

Useful Experience

  • Communications, journalism, media, marketing or related experience.
  • Interest in security, technology, intelligence or social-impact work.
  • A portfolio of digital content, including work created for personal or professional accounts.
  • Basic visual-design or content-production skills.

Working Arrangement

This role is primarily remote, with occasional field-based work where required.

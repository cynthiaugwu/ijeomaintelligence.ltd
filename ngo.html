Solutions

NGOs & Humanitarian

For organisations responsible for staff and programmes operating across locations where local conditions can change quickly and reliable information is uneven.

Operating reality

Field operations rarely happen in one security environment.

Humanitarian and development teams can move between offices, communities, project locations and transport corridors in the same day. Conditions that are manageable in one location may be materially different a short distance away.

The challenge is not simply receiving more alerts. It is maintaining enough current context around people and movement to know what deserves attention.

Where Ijeoma fits

A clearer operating picture around people in the field.

Ijeoma can give authorised teams a more consistent view of the security environment around the places and movements that matter to their operations.

It is intended to complement existing security procedures and local judgement, not replace them.

Location awareness Understand relevant security activity around operational areas without relying on a single information source.

Ijeoma Intelligence opens controlled pilot programme in Nigeria

Controlled testing begins ahead of the public launch of Ijeoma, the company’s consumer safety intelligence platform.

6 November 2026 · Ijeoma Intelligence

Ijeoma Intelligence has opened applications for a controlled pilot programme in Nigeria ahead of the public launch of Ijeoma, its consumer safety intelligence platform.

The pilot is designed to test how the product performs in normal day-to-day use and to gather direct feedback from users before wider release.

Selected participants will use Ijeoma over a defined testing period and provide feedback on the product experience, including setup, location-based safety features, alerts, journey-related functionality and emergency features.

The programme will help Ijeoma Intelligence identify usability issues, test product assumptions and make final improvements ahead of public launch.

Nigeria is Ijeoma Intelligence’s first operating market. The company is building safety intelligence for individuals and organisations operating across complex and changing risk environments.
